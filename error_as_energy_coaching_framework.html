The three laws of growth

Error is signal

Mistakes don't mean you're broken. They reveal the slope — the exact direction your next step should go.

Motion is the goal

Growth isn't a destination. Each small correction accumulates into who you become. You are always mid-journey.

Terrain shapes you

Your environment, habits, and relationships are the landscape. Choose them — they determine where you can go.

Coaching reflection questions

1

Where in your life does a mistake feel like evidence of who you are, rather than information about what to adjust?

2

Think of a time you were "stuck." Looking back, were you actually on a plateau — slowly gathering energy to move?

3

What would you attempt if you truly believed your mistakes were guiding you rather than defining you?

4

Which "landscape" in your life — relationships, work, habits — is shaping your movement in ways you haven't examined?

5

Where are you taking steps that are too large (overwhelm) or too small (no progress)? What's the right step size right now?
